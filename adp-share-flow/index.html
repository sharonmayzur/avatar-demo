ADP Player Share Flow Product Spec & Figma Alignment

This flow allows enterprise users to share individual assets directly from the active playback controls, bypassing manual catalog navigation.

1

Player Trigger (Button States)

Positioned on the right action bar beside Volume, PiP, Settings, and Fullscreen.

• Default: Clean white share icon (3 connected nodes).
• Hover: Expands with a vibrant pink pill background (#ff2d75) and white "SHARE" text.
• Active/Press: Blue accent highlight (#000080).

2

Share Modal Overlay

Dark backdrop blur (rgba(0,0,0,0.75)) with centered card:

• Title: INSPIRE OTHERS. SHARE NOW.
• Channels: Red branded icons for Facebook, Email, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, and MS Teams.
• Close: Top-right 'X' or backdrop click.

3

URL Generation & Deep Linking

Generates a short Branch.io deep link + query parameters:

?assetId=4484118&assetType=1277&play=true

• play=true: Opens directly into player & starts playback.
• play=false: Lands on Series / Asset details page.

4

User Copy Feedback

Clicking COPY LINK copies the URL to the OS clipboard, changes the button label to "COPIED! ✓" (green), and pops an animated confirmation toast.
